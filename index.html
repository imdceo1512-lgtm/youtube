Local Creator Publisher

Local Creator Publisher is a personal Windows workflow for making short educational videos and publishing them to a YouTube channel after its owner grants access.

What it does

The workflow reads public India trend and news feeds, drafts an original explainer with an AI model running on the computer, creates narration and motion graphics locally, and uploads the finished video to the channel selected by its owner.

Google data use

It requests permission to upload videos and read channel details so it can confirm which channel is connected. It does not request Gmail or email access. The tool stores OAuth tokens, upload history, and generated videos on the owner's computer. It sends authorization requests and video uploads to Google and YouTube as needed to perform the requested publishing.

Video topics are selected automatically. The owner authorizes a channel once; the workflow then runs on a local schedule. Public videos are visible on the selected YouTube channel.
