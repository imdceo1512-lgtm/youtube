Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Local Creator Publisher is a personal-use program that runs on its owner's Windows computer. This policy explains how it handles Google and YouTube data.

Data accessed and why

After the owner grants OAuth permission, the program reads YouTube channel identifiers and names to confirm the destination channel. It uploads video files and their titles, descriptions, and tags to the selected YouTube channel. It does not request or read Gmail, email messages, contacts, or Google profile information.

Local storage

OAuth refresh tokens and access tokens are stored in a local secrets folder on the owner's computer. The program also stores generated videos, channel identifiers, video identifiers, trend topics, and run logs locally to publish to the selected channel and avoid repeating recent topics. The owner can delete these files from the computer and revoke access in their Google Account security settings.

Sharing

Authorization and token-refresh requests go to Google. Channel lookup and video uploads go to YouTube through the YouTube Data API. Trend and news lookups request public RSS feeds. Script generation runs with a language model on the local computer; this program does not send Google account data or source material to a third-party AI service.

Videos uploaded with public visibility, including their metadata, are visible to the public on the selected YouTube channel.

Retention and security

Local tokens remain on the owner's computer until deleted or revoked. Video files and run history remain until the owner deletes them. The owner is responsible for keeping the computer and its local files secure.

Contact

This is a personal-use tool. The Google OAuth authorization screen displays the project owner's support contact to the person granting access.
